Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I download videos?

Simply paste the URL of the video you want to download into the input field on our homepage, and click the download button. SocialVault will automatically detect the platform and provide download options.

Which platforms are supported?

We support YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, X (Twitter), Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, and more. We're constantly adding support for new platforms.

Is SocialVault free to use?

Yes! SocialVault offers a free tier with unlimited downloads. We also offer premium plans with additional features like batch downloads, higher quality options, and priority support.

What video quality can I download?

You can download videos in various qualities including 4K, 1080p, 720p, and 480p, depending on the source video's available quality. Premium users get access to the highest quality options.

How fast are the downloads?

Download speeds depend on your internet connection and the source platform. However, SocialVault uses optimized servers to ensure the fastest possible download speeds.
